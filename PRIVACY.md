# Privacy Policy for HBOmax Dark Gradients Remover

Last updated: August 7, 2026

HBOmax Dark Gradients Remover is committed to protecting your privacy. This privacy policy describes how we handle information in our browser extension.

## 1. Data Collection & Transmission
HBOmax Dark Gradients Remover does **NOT** collect, store, or transmit any user data, personal information, or browsing history. All operations are carried out entirely locally on your device.

## 2. Chrome Permissions Used
The extension requests only the minimum permissions necessary to function:
* **`storage`**: Used locally on your device to save your toggle setting (whether the gradient remover is enabled or disabled).
* **`tabs`**: Used strictly to detect if the active tab is viewing Max or HBO Max, and to reload that tab when you toggle the switch so that the setting takes effect immediately. No browsing data or URL history is sent to any server.

## 3. Changes to This Policy
We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted directly to this repository.

## 4. Contact
If you have any questions or feedback regarding this policy, please open an issue on our [GitHub Repository](https://github.com/taahabz/HBOmax-Dark-Gradients-Remover).
